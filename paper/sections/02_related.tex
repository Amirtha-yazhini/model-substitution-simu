\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Detectors by signal.} \emph{Content} detectors compare response text with a reference.
The one-token test~\cite{bruckner2026} asks closed questions (``a number from 1 to 100'') under a
16-token cap and scores the Jensen--Shannon divergence between answer distributions. It covered 165
models for \$34 with an equal error rate of 7.3\%, or 10.6\% at the reduced 8-cell operating point we
use. KBF~\cite{fang2026kbf} probes numeric facts near a model's knowledge boundary, where models of
different sizes in one family disagree, and across 16 production endpoints rejects none of 16
same-reference controls; in mixed-routing simulations it reaches 95\% detection at a substitution
rate of 15\%. IRIS~\cite{iris2026} trains a random forest on 179 visible-string
features of random-number and random-string answers, sizes its own query budget from a pilot, and
estimates the dilution rate.
\emph{Operational} detectors use metadata: GateScope~\cite{lin2026gatescope} audits ten commercial
gateways on content, 25-turn memory, billing residuals and the coefficient of variation of latency.
\emph{Benchmark} detectors~\cite{cai2025} compare accuracy on MMLU, GSM8K, MATH and GPQA; the same
paper finds software-only auditing unreliable and recommends trusted execution environments.
\emph{Reference-model} detectors need more access. RUT~\cite{zhu2025rut} tests whether the ranks of
emitted tokens are uniform under a locally deployed copy of the model, and DiFR~\cite{difr2025}
compares generated tokens with a trusted reference run under the same sampling seed. Provenance methods~\cite{stemma2026} and architecture
inference~\cite{archinfer2026} ask what a model is rather than whether it changed.

\textbf{Replication.} A frozen-threshold holdout study~\cite{holdout2026} matched model lineage by
\texttt{usage.prompt\_tokens}. Development pairs separated perfectly, but on holdout pairs
sensitivity fell to 0.50, and rate limits and missing fields left only 6 of 12 pairs scorable. We
adopt its practice of sealing thresholds before evaluation and apply it to six methods instead of
one.

\textbf{The gap.} Adaptive gateways are not new. Cai et al.~\cite{cai2025} route benchmark queries
honestly, RUT is designed so that its queries look like user traffic, and IRIS shows that keyword
and output-shape filters can spot its probes and proposes rotating disguised probes in response.
Each of these, however, tests evasion against its own probes, and no two detectors were run on
the same data. IRIS is the closest to our setting, since it also handles dilution and audit
budgets. We differ in three ways: one evading gateway applied to every detector, a common replayed
corpus on which all auditors see byte-identical responses, and benign routing scored as a
false-positive test within the same grid. Commercial routers already choose among providers of the same
model automatically~\cite{openrouter_exacto}, so any detector that flags heterogeneity will flag
them.
