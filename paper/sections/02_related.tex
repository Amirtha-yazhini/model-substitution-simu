\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Detectors by signal.} Content based detectors compare response text with a reference. The
Bruckner fingerprint based one-token test~\cite{bruckner2026} asks closed questions (``a number
from 1 to 100'') to detect `fingerprints' under a 16-token cap and scores the Jensen--Shannon
divergence between answer distributions. It covered 165 models for \$34 with an equal error rate
of 7.3\%, or 10.6\% at the reduced 8-cell operating point we use. KBF~\cite{fang2026kbf} probes
numeric facts near a model's knowledge boundary, where models of different sizes in one family
disagree, and reports catching 5--10\% dilution with no false positives across 16 endpoints.
IRIS~\cite{iris2026} trains a random forest on 179 visible-string features and runs a budgeted
sequential plan against OpenRouter models,which includes dilution. Operational based detectors use
metadata: GateScope~\cite{lin2026gatescope} audits ten commercial gateways on content, 25-turn
memory, billing residuals and the coefficient of variation of latency. Benchmark
detectors~\cite{cai2025} compare accuracy on MMLU, GPQA and GSM8K; the same paper finds
software-only auditing unreliable and recommends hardware based trusted execution environments.
RUT~\cite{zhu2025rut} tests whether the ranks of emitted tokens are uniform under a locally
deployed reference, and DiFR~\cite{difr2025} verifies inference from raw logits despite
nondeterminism. Provenance methods~\cite{stemma2026} and architecture
inference~\cite{archinfer2026} ask what a model is rather than whether it changed.

\textbf{Replication.} A frozen-threshold holdout study~\cite{holdout2026} matched model lineage by
\texttt{usage.prompt\_tokens}. Development pairs separated perfectly, but on holdout pairs
sensitivity fell to 0.50, and rate limits and missing fields left only 6 of 12 pairs scorable. We
adopt its practice of sealing thresholds before evaluation and apply it to six methods instead of
one.

\textbf{The gap.}So far every detector was evaluvated against gateways which behaved passively
regardless of the request shape. None of them were tested against a gateway that recognises audit
traffic, no two of them were reun on the same data. IRIS method is the closest one to our setting .
The differnece is the adaptive adversary, a common replayed corpus where all auditors can see
byte-identical responses and a benign routing control. Commerical routers in practice split
traffic across providers by default~\cite{openrouter_exacto} any detector that flags based on this
type of heterogeneity will tend to flag them.
